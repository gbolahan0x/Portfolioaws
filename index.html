About Me

Three paragraphs. No buzzwords. Here's the short version.

I got into tech through a love of software and hardware systems. As a kid I played video games and cracked things open to see how they worked, and that curiosity never left. It's why I'm happiest when I can take a system apart, understand it, and make it work better.

My best work happens at the seam between design and logic: taking something technically correct and making it feel obvious to a first-time visitor. I've shipped a fragrance storefront, a research tool for crypto and opportunities, and four Telegram bots. I care about accessibility too (keyboard focus, reduced motion, readable contrast) because I've watched people bounce off sites that technically "worked."

Right now I'm focused on front-end engineering, DevOps and cloud basics. I'm practising infrastructure automation in public on GitHub, and this portfolio is my first fully deployed site on AWS. The vending machine above is my attempt at making a personal page feel like something rather than a list.
